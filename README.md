# 📦 Supply Chain Management & Predictive Analytics

This project analyzes supply chain performance across multiple companies using data-driven techniques and predictive modeling. The study focuses on key metrics such as order quantity, price volatility, inventory trends, and sales volume to improve operational efficiency and demand forecasting.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The project aims to explore supply chain behavior and build a predictive model capable of forecasting future performance trends. Through statistical analysis and machine learning techniques, the project helps businesses optimize:

* Inventory management
* Demand forecasting
* Procurement planning
* Operational efficiency

---

## 📊 Dataset Insights

The dataset contains multiple supply chain performance indicators, including:

* Order Quantity
* Price of Goods Sold (COGS)
* Daily Sales Volume
* Revenue Growth Rate
* Transportation Efficiency
* Inventory Turnover
* Supplier Relationships

### 🔹 Key Statistics

* Average Order Quantity: **91.73 units**
* Average Price of Goods Sold: **$719.02**
* Price Volatility: **$2.38**
* Average Daily Volume: **43,202+ units**

---

## ⚙️ Methodology

### 🔹 Data Preprocessing

* Missing value handling
* Duplicate removal
* Data cleaning & normalization

### 🔹 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

* Correlation heatmaps
* Histograms & density plots
* Scatter plots
* Parallel coordinate visualization

### 🔹 Feature Engineering

* Correlation-based feature selection
* Creation of optimized predictive features

### 🔹 Predictive Modeling

* Linear Regression Model
* Revenue & trend forecasting
* Model validation using:

  * R-squared Score
  * Mean Squared Error (MSE)
  * Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

---

## 📈 Results & Findings

* Stable order quantities and low price volatility indicate a consistent supply chain environment.
* Strong relationships were observed between:

  * Order fulfillment & customer satisfaction
  * Supplier relationships & supply chain resilience
  * Operational efficiency & revenue growth

### 🔹 Model Performance

* R-squared Score: **0.9611**
* RMSE: **1.7002**
* MSE: **2.8988**

The predictive model demonstrated strong forecasting capability for future supply chain trends.
